Privacy Policy

The Study Shelf · Last updated: 22 August 2026
In short: The Study Shelf collects nothing, sends nothing, and has no account system. It works with the network switched off. Your child's progress is saved only on their own device.

Who this covers

This policy covers the Android application The Study Shelf (package in.studyshelf.app), published by [your name or company], contactable at [your support email].

Information we collect

None. The app has no sign-up, no login, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party SDKs. We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, device identifiers, location or usage data. Nothing you do in the app is transmitted anywhere, because the app makes no network requests at all after installation.

Information stored on the device

To remember where a learner is up to, the app saves the following locally, using Android's own app-private storage:

  • Experience points, coins and daily streak count
  • Which missions in a chapter have been completed
  • Which chapter was opened most recently

This data never leaves the device. It is not backed up to any server we control. It is readable only by this app, and Android deletes it when the app is uninstalled. You can also erase it at any time from Progress & data → Reset all progress inside the app.

Exporting your data

The same screen offers Export progress, which writes a JSON file to your device so you can move progress to another phone. That file is created by you, stays on your device, and is only shared if you choose to share it.

Permissions

The app requests no runtime permissions. It does not use the camera, microphone, contacts, location, storage or notifications.

Children

The app is study material for school students, typically aged 13 to 17. Because it collects no personal information whatsoever, there is nothing for a parent to review, correct or request deletion of beyond the on-device progress described above, which a parent can clear directly in the app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age.

Educational content

Chapter content is based on textbooks published by the National Council of Educational Research and Training (NCERT). This app is an independent study aid and is not endorsed by, affiliated with, or produced in association with NCERT or CBSE.

Changes to this policy

If this app ever begins collecting data, this policy will be updated and the change described here before the feature ships. The date at the top always reflects the current version.
