## App name (30 max)

```
Study Shelf: NCERT Class 9-10
```

29 characters. Keyword-carrying and honest. Alternatives if that name is taken:
`Study Shelf — NCERT Science` (27) · `NCERT Study Shelf` (17)

## Short description (80 max)

```
NCERT chapters you play, not read. Offline, no ads, no sign-up. Class 9 & 10.
```

76 characters. This is the line that shows in search results — it does more work
than the full description. Lead with the difference, close with the reassurance.

## Full description (4000 max)

```
Every chapter in Study Shelf is rebuilt as a set of missions you actually do.

Drag an athlete along a track and watch distance and displacement come apart at
the moment she turns. Cut the root tips off an onion on day three and see the
growth curve flatten. Shade the strip under a velocity-time graph and read the
displacement straight off it. These are things a printed page physically cannot
show you, which is the only reason they are on a screen.

WHAT IS INSIDE
• Class 9 Science, from the new NCERT Exploration textbook
• Class 9 Maths, from Ganita Manjari Part 1
• Class 9 Social Science, from Understanding Society: India and Beyond
• 13 to 16 missions per chapter, each with something to do
• A chapter boss: ten questions drawn from the book's own end-of-chapter
  exercises, with an explanation after every answer
• XP, coins and a daily streak, so coming back tomorrow is worth something

BUILT AROUND THE TEXTBOOK, NOT AROUND IT
The chapter order is the book's order. The vocabulary is the book's vocabulary,
because that is what the exam asks for. The analogies are the book's analogies —
we do not invent new ones, because a made-up metaphor is something a student has
to unlearn later. Optional sidebars stay optional.

You meet a technical term only after you have already seen the idea it names.

WORKS WITH THE PHONE IN AEROPLANE MODE
No sign-up. No account. No ads. No internet needed, ever — not on first launch,
not after. Nothing is collected and nothing is transmitted. Progress is saved on
the device and can be exported to a file if you change phones.

CURRENTLY AVAILABLE
All 13 chapters of Class 9 Science and all 8 chapters of Class 9 Maths, Part 1.
Class 9 Social Science has its first two chapters, with the rest on the way. The
full Class 9 and Class 10 catalogue is listed in the app, and every chapter that
is not built yet is marked Coming soon rather than hidden.

Study Shelf is an independent study aid. It is not endorsed by or affiliated
with NCERT or CBSE.
```

> Trim the "currently available" paragraph as chapters ship. Overstating what is
> in the app is the fastest route to one-star reviews.
